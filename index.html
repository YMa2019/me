Hi, I am Yijia Ma. I received my Ph.D. in Statistics from Florida State University. My research interests broadly lie in functional data analysis, Bayesian modeling, stochastic processes, EEG/fMRI analysis, and computational neuroscience.

My work focuses on developing and applying statistical methods for complex temporal, functional, and high-dimensional biomedical data. I am particularly interested in time-warping models, uncertainty quantification, neural decoding, and multimodal neuroimaging analysis.

Publications

A New Framework for Bayesian Function Registration
Yijia Ma and collaborators
Electronic Journal of Statistics, 2025
paper | code

This work develops a Bayesian framework for function registration by placing stochastic-process priors directly on time-warping functions, enabling posterior inference and uncertainty quantification for phase variability.

A Stochastic Process Framework for Time Warping Functions
Yijia Ma and collaborators
Computational Statistics and Data Analysis, 2024
paper | code

This paper introduces a linear Hilbert-space representation for nonlinear time-warping functions, supporting Gaussian-process modeling, penalized registration, functional boxplots, and outlier detection.

Alpha-Frequency Stimulation Enhances Synchronization of Alpha Oscillations with Default Mode Network Connectivity
Yijia Ma, J. Brown, and collaborators
eNeuro, 2025
paper

This study uses simultaneous EEG-fMRI to examine how alpha-frequency stimulation modulates alpha oscillations and default mode network connectivity.
